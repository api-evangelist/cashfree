TL;DR Debit card MDR is the merchant-side cost of accepting a debit card payment. It is usually deducted before settlement, with GST applied to the fee. For most debit cards, RBI caps MDR at 0.40%, subject to ₹200 per transaction, for merchants with turnover up to ₹20 lakh.
