TL;DR: GST on international transactions depends on the type of transaction. Export of services is generally treated as a zero-rated supply (subject to applicable conditions), while banks, payment gateways, and forex providers may charge 18% GST on their service fees. Understanding these rules can help businesses stay compliant and manage cross-border payments more efficiently.
