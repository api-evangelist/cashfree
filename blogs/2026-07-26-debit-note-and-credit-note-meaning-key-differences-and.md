TL;DR: A debit note is usually issued by the buyer to request an invoice adjustment for damaged goods, short supply, pricing errors, or overcharges. A credit note is generally issued by the seller to confirm the adjustment. Together, they help businesses maintain accurate accounting records, simplify GST compliance, and streamline invoice reconciliation.
