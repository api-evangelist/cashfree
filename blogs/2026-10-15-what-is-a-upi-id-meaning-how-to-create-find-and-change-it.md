Key Takeaways A UPI ID, also called a Virtual Payment Address or VPA, is a unique payment address linked to a bank account. A UPI ID usually contains a name or number followed by a payment handle, such as yourname@oksbi. It is different from a UPI Number, which is an 8 to 10-digit payment alias.
