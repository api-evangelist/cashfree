TL;DR Friendly fraud happens when a customer disputes a legitimate transaction, either due to a genuine misunderstanding or intentional misuse. It can result in lost revenue, chargeback-related costs, operational work, and inventory or fulfilment losses. Clear billing descriptors, transparent policies, proactive customer communication, and strong transaction records can help reduce disputes.
