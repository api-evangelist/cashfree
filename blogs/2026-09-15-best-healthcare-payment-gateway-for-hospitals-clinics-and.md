Patients today book doctor consultations online, schedule diagnostic tests through apps, purchase medicines from online platforms, subscribe to wellness programmes, and pay hospital bills digitally. As healthcare businesses adopt digital-first services, having a reliable healthcare payment gateway becomes increasingly important.
