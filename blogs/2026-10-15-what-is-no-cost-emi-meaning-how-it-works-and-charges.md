Key Takeaways No cost EMI means the customer can split a purchase into instalments while an upfront discount from the merchant, brand or card issuer offsets the disclosed interest. The offer may still involve a processing fee and GST on interest or fees. Customers should compare the final payable amount, not only the monthly EMI.
