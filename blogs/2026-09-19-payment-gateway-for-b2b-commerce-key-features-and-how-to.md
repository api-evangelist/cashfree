Key Takeaways B2B payment infrastructure needs to connect collection, reconciliation, payouts, and settlement. Virtual accounts and payment links can simplify invoice-level collection and reduce manual reconciliation. Recurring B2B models can use mandate-based payment methods such as eNACH and UPI AutoPay.
