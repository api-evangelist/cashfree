TL;DR An ex gratia payment is generally a voluntary payment made as a goodwill gesture without an existing contractual or statutory obligation to pay that specific amount. Employers may offer ex gratia payments during layoffs, emergencies or retirement arrangements. Eligibility and calculation depend on the relevant policy and facts.
