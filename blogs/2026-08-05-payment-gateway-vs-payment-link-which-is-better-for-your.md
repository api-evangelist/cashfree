TL;DR: A payment gateway is ideal for businesses with a website or app that need a seamless checkout and advanced payment management features. A payment link is best for collecting payments quickly through WhatsApp, email, SMS, or invoices without technical integration. Many businesses use both to provide customers with greater payment flexibility.
