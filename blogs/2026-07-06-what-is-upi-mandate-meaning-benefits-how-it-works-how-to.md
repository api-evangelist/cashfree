A UPI mandate is a one-time authorization that enables automatic recurring payments from your UPI-linked bank account. UPI has transformed digital payments in India, making money transfers fast and convenient. Beyond instant payments, it also enables UPI Mandates, a feature that automates recurring payments like EMIs, subscriptions, insurance premiums, SIPs, and utility bills.
