TL;DR Indian D2C brands increasingly sell in more than one place. An online store handles website orders, but exhibitions, pop-ups, craft markets, and retail counters need a physical payment solution too. Buying or renting a dedicated POS machine for occasional offline selling is expensive and inconvenient.
