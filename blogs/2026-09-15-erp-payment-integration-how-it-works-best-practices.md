Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems bring core business operations such as finance, accounting, inventory, sales, and customer management into one system. But payment processing often sits outside the ERP, creating another layer of manual work for finance and operations teams. ERP payment integration connects the payment layer with the ERP and surrounding business systems.
