Merchant QR codes enable businesses to accept instant UPI and digital payments with a simple scan, reducing cash handling, speeding up checkout, and improving the customer experience. They are easy to set up, cost-effective, and suitable for businesses of all sizes. Merchant QR code payment shows up most clearly in everyday moments at the counter.
