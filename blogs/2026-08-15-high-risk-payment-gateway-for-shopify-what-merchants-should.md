High-risk Shopify merchants often face more scrutiny when choosing a payment gateway. Approval requirements, reserves, settlement timelines, fraud controls, and supported business categories can vary significantly between providers. This makes gateway selection especially important for merchants operating in categories with higher dispute, fraud, regulatory, or compliance risk.
