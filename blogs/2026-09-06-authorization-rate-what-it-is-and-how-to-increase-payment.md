Not every payment attempt results in a successful transaction. A customer may have sufficient funds and still face a decline because of issuer-bank checks, incorrect transaction data, authentication issues, fraud controls, routing problems, or temporary technical failures. This is where authorization rate becomes an important payment performance metric.
